What happens when you hold the button

  1. QuoteEach coin you ticked gets a Jupiter route to SOL for its full balance, 3% max slippage. Coins with no route are left alone and listed.
  2. BuildOne transaction per coin: the swap, then the emptied token account is closed so its rent comes back to you. Empty accounts you already had are closed in batches of 20.
  3. Sign onceYour wallet shows every transaction in one prompt. Approve once and they are all sent.
  4. LandEach transaction is tracked until it confirms, with its Solscan link. A failed one never blocks the others.

0.5% of each swap's minimum SOL out goes to the EJECT treasury with an EJECT memo, and the treasury buys back $EJECT every hour. Rent from closed accounts is never charged. Up to 20 coins per eject. Stablecoins are never ticked for you.
